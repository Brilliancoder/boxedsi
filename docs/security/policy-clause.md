# Policy clause

A clause for the company's AI policy. Replace the bracketed parts.

> **Use of AI services with work data.** Employees may use [approved AI services and account types] for work tasks only through boxed.si, with the company profile [no-confidential] and these rules:
>
> 1. Keys, passwords and tokens are never sent; boxed masks the ones it detects at every level.
> 2. Personal data of customers and staff is sent only masked, at level 1 or higher; HR and health data only to [company plan or local model] and never to personal accounts.
> 3. Financial results, deal terms and strategy are sent only at level 3 or higher, or as level 4 (task and table schema only).
> 4. Training on chats must be off in every account used for work; each employee records the setting in boxed, which marks a check as due after 30 days (a due mark shown when the side panel or `boxed check list` is opened) and shows the date of the last answer.
> 5. Text is never typed or pasted straight into an AI website with work data outside boxed.
> 6. The boxed journal is kept for [N months] and exported to [the security team or SIEM] every [period].
> 7. At onboarding each employee runs `boxed audit`; real keys it finds in saved session records are rotated within [N days].
>
> Exceptions need written approval from [role].
